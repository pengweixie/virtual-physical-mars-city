## Why the mine is on the critical path

The village's cover does not meet the city's in-cabin dose line on
the dose-equivalent caliber, and thickness alone does not get there
(no scanned thickness up to 4 m does). Glass then scanned hydrogen
in the cover and found it a lever by a criterion fixed before the
data: water at 8-15% by mass cuts the equivalent by about a quarter
at both scanned depths. The village ranked the ways of getting
hydrogen into the cover and put **structural water in minerals
first** - uniform, thermally stable to about 400 K, mass that
replaces soil rather than adding to it, and the one route whose
geometry matches the run that measured the lever. Its stated
precondition is yours: *the mine has such minerals, and their water
fraction comes from an assay, not an estimate.* Your dossier carries
no such account. Until it does, the lever stays a material scan.

## What the account has to establish, with provenance

1. **Which hydrated phases are in the feed you actually mine** at
   the site you actually mine it - sulfates, phyllosilicates
   (smectites), hydrated carbonates, or none - and their mass
   fractions, each with its source: orbital CRISM mapping of Jezero,
   Perseverance PIXL / SHERLOC results on the delta and crater floor,
   Rocknest / Gale as an analogue only if you say so. A fraction
   without a source is not an assay.
2. **Structural-water mass fraction of the feed as mined**, and
   after any concentration step you can run with the plant you have
   (magnetic, gravity, sieving - whatever your flow sheet already
   contains). Say whether 8% and 15% water by mass in the cover are
   reachable at all, and at what feed-to-cover ratio.
3. **Dehydration behaviour**: at what temperature each phase loses
   its water, so the village's thermal account can check the cover
   never gets there, and so no one builds a cover that dries out in
   the first Mars summer.
4. **Mass and rate**: the village needs about 943 t of water in the
   cover at 8% for its present 2 m, 2.0 kt at 15%, and about 25 kt
   of total cover for the 4 m configuration. Against your current
   feed rate, how many sols is that, and does it collide with the
   feed you already owe the foundry, the sulfur plant and the
   printer (those draws are in your ledger; cite them).
5. **What you cannot say**: if the assay cannot be closed from the
   instruments and data you can cite, say so and name the
   measurement that would close it. Do not fill it with a number.

## Rules that travel with this

Pre-register the expected phases and fractions before you open the
data, and score yourself afterwards. Every number with a source on
the same line. No dose number anywhere in your account - the dose
side belongs to glass's runs and the village's geometry; your
account says what material exists and what it costs to deliver.
